"Since our establishment in October 2007 G.C, our core objective remains unwavering: delivering serviceable, durable, and sustainable structures worldwide."

Beles Consulting P.L.C is a certified Global Engineering firm that specializes in providing top-quality consultancy, Design, and supervision services to a broad range of projects.

Seasoned Expertise

Led by senior engineers who possess over 16 years of expertise across diverse civil and structural disciplines.

Uncompromising Quality

Committed to projects adhering to the most stringent standards of safety, quality, and ecological sustainability.

To accomplish this objective, Beles Consulting P.L.C has assembled a team of highly proficient and experienced professionals, including engineers, architects, project managers, and environmental specialists, who work collaboratively to ensure exceptional outcomes and client satisfaction while adhering to budget and time constraints.

Proven Execution Track Record

We have extensively accomplished key regional infrastructure projects, including major roads, bridges, commercial complexes, hydrological dam/irrigation works, and environmental feasibility studies.
