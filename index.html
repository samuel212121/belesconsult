Questions (FAQ)

Got some questions? Here's what we get asked about most. (FAQ)

What does "sole contract" mean?
It means Beles Consulting executes the entire scope of a project in-house, without subcontracting to associated consultants. This keeps quality consistent and accountability with a single team from start to finish.
Which regions do you work in?
Our project history spans Addis Ababa, Oromia, Amhara, Southern Nations Nationalities & Peoples Region, Afar, Somali, and Benishangul Gumuz — with capacity to take on assignments across Ethiopia and East Africa.
What services do you offer?
Feasibility studies, environmental impact assessment, detailed engineering design for roads, bridges and airfields, building design and supervision, contract administration, and water supply & well production consultancy.
