Office & Headquarters

Establishing corporate administrative dominance and long-term planning frameworks to steer our central engineering departments with absolute control.

Architectural Planning Board

Planned G+8 Head Office

BELES Consulting P.L.C. has meticulously planned the construction of a G+8 mixed-use building for its permanent head office in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. This strategic decision takes into account prevailing cost advantages and aims to achieve highly efficient administrative performance and elite-quality output across all infrastructure projects undertaken by the company.

The land for this proposed development is currently in the final stages of acquisition. Once completed, the newly constructed headquarters will serve as the permanent, fully controlled seat of our management team. It will house our core administrative departments, accounting systems, financial hubs, and detailed technical engineering divisions together under a unified, state-of-the-art workspace.

This spatial integration ensures that our team operates in a highly organized, completely transparent, and professional manner. It guarantees complete operational control and robust, streamlined communications between design offices and on-site field engineers.
